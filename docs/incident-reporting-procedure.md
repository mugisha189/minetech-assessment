# Incident Reporting Procedure

## What Must Be Reported

All of the following must be reported, regardless of severity:
- Injuries (including near-misses and first-aid cases)
- Equipment damage or failure
- Environmental spills or releases
- Security breaches
- Hazardous conditions observed on site

A near-miss is any unplanned event that did not result in injury or damage but had the potential to do so. Near-misses are treated with the same priority as actual incidents.

## Immediate Response

1. Ensure your own safety first. Do not enter an unsafe area to assist.
2. Call emergency services if there is a risk to life: Site Emergency Line — ext. 000.
3. Provide first aid only if you are trained and it is safe to do so.
4. Preserve the scene. Do not move equipment or disturb evidence unless required for safety.
5. Notify your supervisor immediately.

## Filing a Report

All incidents must be recorded in the Incident Management System (IMS) within 4 hours of occurrence. The report must include:

- Date, time, and exact location
- Names of all persons involved or witnessing the incident
- Description of what happened (factual, first-person)
- Equipment or materials involved
- Immediate actions taken
- Potential contributing factors (not causes — that is determined in the investigation)

Photographs and sketches should be attached where possible.

## Investigation

A formal investigation is required for all incidents classified as serious (Lost Time Injury, significant property damage, or environmental release). Investigations are led by the Safety Officer with involvement from the relevant supervisor.

The investigation report must be completed within 5 business days and submitted to the Site Manager and the HSE team. Root cause analysis uses the 5-Why methodology as a minimum.

## Corrective Actions

All investigations produce a corrective action register. Actions are assigned to named owners with due dates. Outstanding actions are reviewed at the weekly safety meeting. Overdue actions are escalated to the Site Manager.

## Confidentiality

Incident reports are used for safety improvement only. They are not used for disciplinary purposes except in cases of deliberate misconduct or gross negligence.
