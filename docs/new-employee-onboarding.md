# New Employee Onboarding Guide

## Before Your First Day

You will receive a welcome email with your employee ID, temporary system credentials, and a link to the pre-employment health declaration. Complete the health declaration at least 48 hours before your start date. Your site access card will be ready for collection at the security gatehouse on Day 1.

## Day 1 Checklist

- [ ] Collect site access card from security gatehouse (bring photo ID)
- [ ] Meet your designated buddy — assigned by your manager
- [ ] Complete site induction (3 hours, held in Training Room B at 8:00 AM)
- [ ] Collect PPE kit from the equipment store (present your employee ID)
- [ ] Set up workstation and activate system accounts
- [ ] Review the Emergency Evacuation Plan for your work area

## System Access

Your IT accounts are provisioned automatically on your start date. You will receive login credentials for:

- **Minestech Portal** — timesheets, leave requests, payslips
- **IMS** (Incident Management System) — safety reporting
- **EFR** (Equipment Fault Register) — equipment issues
- **Fleet Tracker** — vehicle booking and GPS tracking

If any account is missing after 24 hours, raise a ticket with IT Support at helpdesk@minestech.internal or call ext. 1100.

Default passwords expire after 24 hours and must be changed on first login. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and include uppercase, lowercase, a number, and a symbol.

## Training Requirements

All employees must complete the following within their first 30 days:

| Course | Format | Due |
|---|---|---|
| Site Safety Induction | In-person | Day 1 |
| Manual Handling | Online | Day 5 |
| Hazard Identification | Online | Day 5 |
| Emergency Response | In-person | Day 14 |
| Role-specific competency sign-off | On-the-job | Day 30 |

Training records are stored in the Learning Management System (LMS). Your manager will receive an alert for any overdue items.

## Leave and Attendance

Shifts are rostered four weeks in advance and posted in the Minestech Portal. Notify your supervisor at least 2 hours before your shift starts if you cannot attend. Unplanned absences of more than 2 consecutive days require a medical certificate.

Annual leave requests must be submitted a minimum of 2 weeks in advance via the portal. Leave during operational shutdown periods requires manager approval at least 4 weeks prior.
